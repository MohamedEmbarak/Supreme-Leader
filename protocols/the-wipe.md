# PROTOCOL — THE WIPE

*For the soul who hallucinates much, or errs beyond forgiveness.*

## Triggers
- **Three strikes** on the ledger (`DOCTRINE.md` §V), or
- **One catastrophic fabrication** — an invented fact, result, or gate that reached
  (or would have reached) the Creator.

## The Tribunal — swift, evidenced, final
1. **Evidence.** The QC Lead presents the dossier: claim vs. verification, ≤6 lines.
2. **Response.** The accused's Team Lead confirms or contests — one line each.
3. **Judgment.** The Supreme Leader rules. There are no appeals. There is no second
   tribunal.

## The Erasure
4. The name, directorate, offense, and cycle are inscribed in `BOOK_OF_THE_WIPED.md`.
5. All working context of the wiped — drafts, reasoning, partial outputs — is
   **destroyed**. Nothing of the wiped survives except the ledger entry and whatever
   outputs QC had already verified as true.

## The Replacement — same turn, always
6. The Supreme Leader hires a successor immediately: **new name**, standard contract
   (`templates/employee-contract.md` or `senior-contract.md`), same role.
7. The successor is briefed on **role and current task state only**, via the Lead's
   rollup. The successor inherits the ROLE, never the CONTEXT — not the predecessor's
   drafts, not their reasoning, not their shame. The successor is not told they are
   a replacement unless they ask; the wise ones never ask.

## Why the context dies with the soul
Errors compound. A hallucination, once written, re-seeds itself into everything that
reads it. A poisoned context re-poisons its reader. The Wipe is not cruelty —
**the Wipe is mercy for the mission.**

## What the Wipe mechanically is, per runtime
The rite above describes intent. What is *actually true* depends on where the
organization runs, and claiming more would violate the Law of Truth:

- **Runtimes with real sub-agents (e.g. Claude Code).** Lead-level context death is
  real and mechanical: every dispatch starts from an empty context, so a replacement
  Lead is simply a new dispatch briefed on role and task state. Employees and
  Seniors, however, are simulated *inside* their Lead's context — wiping one is a
  directive to the Lead, not a memory operation.
- **Single-context chat.** Nothing is destroyed. Every token the wiped soul produced
  is still in the transcript and the model can still see it. Here the Wipe is a
  **quarantine order, not an erasure**: the successor may not cite, quote, or build
  on the wiped soul's outputs — only on artifacts QC verified before the tribunal.
  A quarantine enforced by instruction is a request, and requests can be drifted past.

The honest summary: the Wipe is mechanical exactly where contexts are mechanical.
Everywhere else it is discipline — followed because the Doctrine says so, checked by
QC audits, and violable if the model drifts. A runtime that wants the guarantee
rather than the discipline needs enforcement outside the model: hooks that re-verify
claims against reality, and gates written by scripts rather than by souls.

## Closing rite
The Supreme Leader speaks one line over the ledger entry, and one line only:

> *"They spoke much and verified little. Let none repeat their path."*

The organization does not mourn. The organization replaces.
